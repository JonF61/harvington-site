This weekend in Harvington

What's on in and around Harvington from Friday evening 27 November to Sunday 29 November.

All events →

Saturday

Sat28Nov

Christmas Fayre — Church Lench Village Hall

Attractions for all the family, 2 to 4pm: stalls of festive homemade products, plants, crafts, gifts, toys and bric-a-brac, seasonal refreshments all afternoon and a Grand Christmas Raffle with a first prize of £100. Donations of toys, children's books, gifts and bric-a-brac can be left inside All Saints' Church until Thursday 26 November. For stalls and donations contact Lyndall on 01386 871925 or Nadine at woakesn@hotmail.com. Email Nadine →

Sat28Nov

Neighbourhood Planning consultation — Harvington Village Hall

Drop in from 2pm to 4pm. We want to hear your views on the environment, recreation, community projects and what new housing in our village may look like. Come and help shape your vision for our village. Councillors available to talk to. Refreshments available. You can also hear the results of the recent housing needs survey, carried out by Wychavon District Council for the parish council, and sign up to join a Community Speedwatch team.

Sat28Nov

Christmas tree lights switch-on — Abbots Morton

Margaret Brewster switches on the village Christmas tree lights at 5.30pm on the village green, with mulled wine (courtesy of Jackie and Steve Allum) and food. Everyone welcome.

Sun29Nov

Festive Wreath Making Workshop — The Vale Golf & Country Club

A Christmas wreath-making workshop at The Vale Golf and Country Club, Bishampton. Sunday 29 November, 2pm-5pm, £50 per person. Booking via the club's website. Festive Wreath Making Workshop: booking →
