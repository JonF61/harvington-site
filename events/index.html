Neighbourhood Planning consultation — Harvington Village Hall

Drop in from 2pm to 4pm. We want to hear your views on the environment, recreation, community projects and what new housing in our village may look like. Come and help shape your vision for our village. Councillors available to talk to. Refreshments available. You can also hear the results of the recent housing needs survey, carried out by Wychavon District Council for the parish council, and sign up to join a Community Speedwatch team.

Sun6Dec

Handbell ringing for the Air Ambulance — St James' Harvington Handbell Ringers

From 6 December until Christmas Eve the ringers play 24 engagements, mostly at supermarket doors, with every penny going to Midlands Air Ambulance Charity and Warwickshire & Northamptonshire Air Ambulance. Evesham: Morrisons Wed 9 and Thu 17 Dec, 5–8pm; Tesco Sun 13 Dec, 10am–1pm and Wed 16 Dec, 5–8pm. Stratford-upon-Avon: Waitrose Thu 10 Dec, 5–8pm and Sat 12 and Tue 22 Dec, 10am–1pm; Tesco Tue 15 Dec, 10am–1pm and Sun 20 Dec, 1–4pm; Morrisons Fri 18 Dec, 10am–1pm and Mon 21 Dec, 1–4pm. Alcester: Waitrose Fri 11 Dec, 2–5pm and Sat 19 and Wed 23 Dec, 10am–1pm. In Harvington: St James' Church, Sun 6 Dec at 10am, and Ragley Road on Christmas Eve, 6.30–7pm. Listen out for the bells and give what you can.
