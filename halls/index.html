Harvington Village Hall and Norton and Lenchwick Parish Hall

Harvington and Norton each have a village hall, available for regular groups and one-off events.

Harvington Village Hall

On Village Street, Harvington. Regular users include Harvey Bears Nursery and Pre-School, exercise classes, Girlguiding groups and village clubs. See Regular activities for days and times.

Hire the hall

Email harvingtonvillagehall@gmail.com or call the Booking Secretary on 07436 118644. Hire rates are on the noticeboard outside the hall.

Norton and Lenchwick Parish Hall

At 2 Byrd Row, Norton, the hall for Norton and Lenchwick. Regular users include St Egwin's Craft Group, a fortnightly coffee morning and the u3a's short mat bowls, and it has table tennis equipment for anyone wanting to start a group. See Regular activities for days and times.

Hire the hall

Call the Bookings Administrator on 01386 462884.
