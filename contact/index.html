Send us something

Have an event, notice or piece of news? Email it to website@harvingtonvillage.org.uk and we'll put it on the website and in Thursday's newsletter.

The area we cover

Harvington, Norton and Lenchwick, and neighbouring villages including the Lenches and Abbots Morton, plus events in Evesham and nearby that many villagers would want to know about.

What we leave out

  • Anything party-political.
  • Advertising for businesses.
  • Small ads: things for sale or wanted, lettings, jobs and personal services.
  • Anything anonymous, or that we can't check. Please give your name, or the group you're sending for.

Deadline

Anything in by 6pm on Wednesday goes in that Thursday's newsletter. Later items are looked at for the week after.

What to tell us

A few lines saying:

  • What it is
  • Where it's happening (which village, and the venue)
  • When (the date and the time)
  • How much it costs, or that it's free
  • Who to ask (a name, email address or phone number)

Posters and photos are welcome: photos (JPEG, PNG or straight from an iPhone) or a flyer as a PDF. We can't open Word or Publisher files, so please save them as a PDF first.

When things appear

  • Events go on the website once we've added them, and into the newsletter no sooner than two weeks before the day. A big event may also get one early "save the date" mention.
  • An event stays on the website until its date.
  • An item appears in the newsletter three times at most.
  • A poster or photo is shown twice at most.
  • Sending an item again doesn't start these counts afresh.

Photos of people

Please only send photos you're allowed to share. Everyone in them should be happy to be published, and children need a parent's or guardian's agreement.

What happens next

We'll reply to say your email has arrived. If something's missing we'll ask; if we haven't heard back by 6pm on Wednesday, we'll go ahead with what we have.

Want it published word for word? Say "please use my wording" and we'll change nothing but obvious typos and broken links.

Otherwise we may shorten or tidy items, and we decide what goes in.
