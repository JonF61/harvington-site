Harvington bin collection days

When the bins go out in Harvington, Norton and Lenchwick, and which bin takes what.

Page updated Saturday, 28 November 2026

Next collections

Your next collection should appear here. If it doesn't, look up your day on Wychavon's Bin Hub.

Put your bin out by 7am on collection day, at the edge of your property by the road, with the lid shut: a bin too full to close may not be emptied.

Which bin for what

Green bin: recycling

Put everything in loose, rinsed and dry. Recycling tied up in black sacks won't be taken, even inside the green bin.

Tins, cans and foil

Food, drink and pet food tins and cans, empty aerosols, biscuit and sweet tins, and metal lids from jars. Clean foil and foil trays (squash foil into a ball so it doesn't get lost) and metal tubes such as tomato purée.

Not pans, cutlery or tools, foil-lined pouches or coffee pods. Leave cans unflattened.

Glass bottles and jars

Any colour, with lids and labels left on. Perfume bottles and cosmetic jars are fine too.

Not drinking glasses, ovenware, window glass or nail varnish bottles. Households on sacks should take glass to a bank or the tip instead.

Plastic bottles, pots, tubs and trays

Bottles from drinks, milk, cleaning products and toiletries; yoghurt pots and spread tubs; food trays and plant pots. Squeezy tubes like toothpaste, emptied and with the cap back on.

Not black plastic, carrier bags or film, pumps and trigger sprays, or toys and other hard plastics. Put bottle tops back on rather than in loose.

Paper

Newspapers and magazines, catalogues, post and envelopes, junk mail and cards. Shredded paper only when it's tucked inside an envelope or a cereal box.

Not glitter cards, shiny metallic wrap, labels or anything stained with food.

Cardboard

Boxes and packaging, cereal and pet food boxes, food sleeves, egg boxes and the tubes from kitchen and toilet rolls, all flattened.

Remove plastic film and polystyrene first. Greasy pizza boxes belong in the black bin.

Cartons

Drinks, soup, sauce and custard cartons, flattened. Spouts can stay; loose lids can go in separately.

Black bin: general rubbish

Everything that can't be recycled, food waste included: Wychavon doesn't collect food separately yet.

That covers black plastic, cling film and other soft plastic, crisp and sweet wrappers, foil-lined pouches, coffee pods, drinking glasses, ovenware, pumps and trigger sprays.

Until Wychavon starts collecting plastic film, which it plans for spring 2027, bigger supermarkets take it at their carrier bag recycling points.

Next to the black bin

Batteries

Bag them in a small clear bag and leave it on the lid of your black bin; crews keep them apart on the lorry. Most supermarkets also collect them.

Small electricals

Anything with a plug, battery or crossed-out wheelie bin sign. Leave it on or beside the bin, never inside it or in the recycling.

Clothes and textiles

Clean, dry clothes, towels and bedding in a well-tied bag beside the bin to keep the rain out. Things in good condition are better given to a charity shop.

Some homes have sacks instead of wheelie bins; they're collected on the same days.

Recycling banks

Handy if you're on sacks or have more than fits in your green bin.

  • The Fish & Anchor, Evesham Road, Offenham: glass
  • Oat Street, Evesham: glass and cans
  • Morrisons, Springfield Drive, Evesham: glass and cans
  • Tesco, Worcester Road, Evesham: glass and cans

The tip

The nearest household recycling centre is Hill & Moor, Piddle Brook Lane, Wyre Piddle WR10 2LW.

Open 8am to 6pm Monday to Saturday and 9am to 1.30pm on Sunday; hours change with the seasons, so check before you go. Cars don't need to book, but bring proof that you live in Worcestershire.

If you're taking a van, trailer or pickup you'll need an e-permit from Worcestershire County Council first.

Based on Wychavon District Council and Worcestershire County Council information. Collection days can move, especially over Christmas and New Year. For a particular item, try Wychavon's A to Z recycling guide, and if anything here looks wrong, please email website@harvingtonvillage.org.uk.
