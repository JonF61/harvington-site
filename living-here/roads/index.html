Roads

Closures and roadworks on and around Harvington's roads, from one.network, updated several times a day. The dates are the contractors' own plans and often slip either way, so check one.network before you set off.

    Badgers Hill, Sheriffs LenchClosure

    Sheriffs Lench · From Tue 27 Oct

    Badgers Hill to Handgate Lane will be closed from Tuesday 27 October to Sunday 29 November for Severn Trent Water streetworks, with a diversion via Handgate Lane, Church Lench.

    Abbots Morton Road, Abbots MortonClosure

    Abbots Morton · From Mon 16 Nov

    Abbots Morton Road will be closed for carriageway patching and resurfacing on 3 days between Monday 16 and Tuesday 24 November, ahead of surface dressing by Worcestershire County Council. A diversion will use Alcester Road, and delays are likely.

    Openreach works — Alcester Road, RadfordRoadworks

    Radford · From Sat 21 Nov

    Openreach is carrying out works on Alcester Road, Radford, on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November. They won't take up any of the road, so delays are unlikely.
