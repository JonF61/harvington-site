Getting around: buses from Harvington to Evesham

Buses are few, so it pays to plan ahead. Timetables change, so the links below go to live times rather than copying them here.

Plan a journeyTraveline Live bus timesbustimes.org TrainsNational Rail RoadworksThis week's closures

Buses

28 (Stagecoach)

Harvington to Evesham, and the other way to Bidford and Stratford-upon-Avon. About hourly through the day. Timetable

Stops: Golden Cross, Coach & Horses, First School, Brickyard Cottages.

564 family (LMS Travel)

Inkberrow and the Lenches through Norton to Evesham railway station and bus station; some journeys run via Pershore. Monday to Saturday, a few journeys a day. Timetable

Stops: Norton.

Lenchwick has no bus stop of its own; the nearest are in Norton and Harvington.

Worcestershire on Demand

A bookable minibus run by the county council. Book by app or phone up to five days ahead; it picks up from set points. Monday to Saturday, 7am to 7pm; £1 a journey within the Wychavon Zone until 31 March 2028 (otherwise £2.50), free with a bus pass. Phone 01905 846180 or get the app.

Check the zone map to see whether your address is covered. Zone map and details

Community transport

Volunteer drivers, door to door, for appointments, shopping and visits. No age limit; register first, and book ahead.
