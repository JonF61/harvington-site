Harvington CofE First School and other schools

Children in Harvington, Norton and Lenchwick go through Worcestershire's three stages: first school from Reception to Year 5, middle school for Years 6 to 8, then high school from Year 9.

  • Nursery 2–4
  • First 4–10
  • Middle 10–13
  • High 13–18

Middle and high schools

Harvington, Norton and Lenchwick are in the county's Evesham area. Harvington First's linked middle school is St Egwin's, which in turn links to Prince Henry's High, but your catchment schools depend on your address. Check your address with the county.

Applying for a place

Places at every state school, academies included, are applied for through Worcestershire County Council's online form.

Getting to school

Your child may get free travel to their catchment or nearest suitable school if it is more than 2 miles away (under 8) or 3 miles away (8 and over), measured along the shortest walking route.
