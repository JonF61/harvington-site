Parish councils

Harvington Parish Council

Harvington

Meets on the second Wednesday of every month except August, at 7.45pm in Harvington Village Hall. Members of the public are welcome to attend.

Norton and Lenchwick Parish Council

Norton and Lenchwick

Meets every two months, usually on the first Thursday, at 7.30pm in Norton and Lenchwick Village Hall. Dates, agendas and minutes are on the council's website.

Who does what

Parish council

Gives its view on planning applications and looks after village matters such as open spaces and noticeboards.

Wychavon District

Decides planning applications and runs bin collections, council tax, housing and licensing.

Worcestershire County

Roads and pavements, potholes, streetlights, public footpaths, schools, libraries and social care.
