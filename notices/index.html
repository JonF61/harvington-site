Staying safe at home — police advice, Rous Lench Village Hall

After a recent incident in which someone trespassed in a garden in the villages and looked through the windows, the Safer Neighbourhood Team has been visiting residents.

More

Black Friday and £100 energy payment scam warning

West Mercia Police's November fraud bulletin warns that fake Black Friday deals are being offered all month. Buy only from retailers you trust, check website addresses carefully, avoid links in unexpected emails and be wary of pressure to act quickly or pay by bank transfer.

More

Criminals may also use the one-off £100 energy payment due to eligible households in January 2027: only your own council will contact you about it, so never give bank details to anyone who calls, texts or emails. If you have been scammed, contact Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040.

Neighbourhood Watch: winter burglary and scam advice

Neighbourhood Watch says burglaries tend to rise as the evenings draw in, and asks residents to look out for vulnerable neighbours. With more Christmas parcels arriving, have deliveries left somewhere hidden, with a neighbour or at a collection point rather than on the doorstep.

More

Shoppers are also asked to watch out for scams around Black Friday, which is on 27 November this year.

Ofsted success for Fledgling Explorers nursery, Harvington

Fledgling Explorers nursery has been inspected under Ofsted's new framework, and achieved Strong Standards in Children's Welfare and Well-being and in Behaviour, Attitudes and Establishing Routines, and Expected Standards in the four other areas.

More

The report highlights the nursery's warm, nurturing and well-organised environment, how well the staff know each child, and how quickly children form secure attachments.
