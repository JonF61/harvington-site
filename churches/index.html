Harvington church services

Harvington's parish church is St James the Great in Church Street, and Norton's is St Egwin's on Evesham Road. They share a Sunday rota with the churches at Church Lench, Rous Lench and Abbots Morton, all part of the ARCH Benefice, so the place and time of each service move from week to week.
